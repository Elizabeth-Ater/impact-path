About

Elizabeth Ater is a Software Engineer with a particular interest in education and women's empowerment. She built ImpactPath to help incoming university students identify a cause worth acting on early in their academic journey, using an interactive quiz to connect personal interests to one of four Global Citizenship & Goals pillars.

Elizabeth believes that thoughtful software can lower the barrier between good intentions and real action whether that means pointing a student toward a tutoring society, a mentorship circle, or a cause they hadn't yet considered. Her work on this project combines technical implementation with an interest in how technology can widen access to education and support opportunity for women in STEM and beyond.
